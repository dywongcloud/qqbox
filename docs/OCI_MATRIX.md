# Operational OCI construct matrix

This is the acceptance surface for "100% of the operational OCI constructs". Status
columns: **Go fork** (as cloned, HEAD `5fb67db`) and **Rust port** (this tree).
Evidence for the Go column is the adversarial recon receipt
`1794974373760-231-fae90a6b2abefafa` and the exhaustive literal scans listed at the
end. Every Rust row is exercised by a live CLI/API witness.

remains out of contract by design, as it is in the Go fork's `VNET.md:19-26`.
